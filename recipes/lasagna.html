Lasagna Recipe

Recipe

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, and cheese. Here's a simple recipe to make delicious lasagna at home.

Ingredients:

  1. 12 lasagna noodles
  2. 1 pound ground beef
  3. 1 onion, chopped
  4. 2 cloves garlic, minced
  5. 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce
  6. 1 (15-ounce) container ricotta cheese
  7. 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  8. 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  9. 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
  10. Salt and pepper to taste
