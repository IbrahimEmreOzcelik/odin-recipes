Pizza Recipe

Recipe

Pizza is a beloved Italian dish that has become popular worldwide. Here's a simple recipe to make delicious pizza at home.

Ingredients:

  1. 1 pound pizza dough
  2. 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  3. 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  4. Your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, etc.)
  5. 1 tablespoon olive oil
  6. 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  7. Salt and pepper to taste
