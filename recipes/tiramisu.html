Tiramissu Recipe

Recipe

Tiramissu is a classic Italian dessert made with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and a creamy mascarpone mixture. Here's a simple recipe to make delicious tiramissu at home.

Ingredients:

  1. 6 large egg yolks
  2. 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  3. 1 pound mascarpone cheese
  4. 1/2 cup strong brewed coffee, cooled
  5. 2 tablespoons Grand Marnier or other orange liqueur
  6. 2 dozen ladyfingers
  7. 1/2 cup cocoa powder for dusting
